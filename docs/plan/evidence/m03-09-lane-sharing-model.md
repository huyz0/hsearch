# M03.09 lane-sharing model and revocation search

`provisional`, **modelled, not measured**. Generated by `python3 docs/plan/tools/lane_model.py` from the `lane_sharing` block of [cost-calibration-inputs.json](../cost-calibration-inputs.json), which holds every assumption (10M indexes, 10% active, 5% BYOK, 1,000-index lane groups, 2 retained objects per lane, the `object-resilient` profile's 250 ms wait). It reuses the M02.07 batcher and illustrative rate card ([m02-07-cost-calibration.md](m02-07-cost-calibration.md)); the decision is in [M03.09-commit-domain-sharing.md](../decisions/M03.09-commit-domain-sharing.md).

Revocation search (one lane, indexes a and b, histories of 5 operations ending in recovery):
- `per_index_dek`: safe over 625 histories.
- `lane_dek_default`: safe over 256 histories (neither index has a key of its own to revoke separately).
- `lane_dek_a_byok` (deliberately broken control): unsafe, counterexample ['append_a', 'append_a', 'append_b', 'revoke_key_a', 'recover']: record of index b readable=False but its own key revoked=False.

Not done: measured request counts (M03.08), lane re-establish time after a poison, recovery unwrap bound for option C, the M03.04 authority model, a real trace, and any real KMS or provider number. The model assumes uniform sharing groups; real group assignment (by policy tuple and arrival) may differ.
