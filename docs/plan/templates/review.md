## Findings

An empty list is valid. For each actionable finding give severity, exact location, concrete failing history/input, violated contract and smallest verification that can disprove the finding. Separate correctness from preference. Identify weak or dependent test oracles.

## Verdict

State pass or blocking findings and scope reviewed. Bind the verdict to the final tree and evidence hashes. Follow-up verification reads the delta and unresolved findings. At the round cap, unresolved blockers require split/redesign; they cannot be waived by running out of tokens.
