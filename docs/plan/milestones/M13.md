# M13: Catalog foundation, elasticity and fleet scaling

Generated from `tasks.json`; edit the ledger and run `python docs/plan/tools/plan.py render`.

Status: **planned**. Owner role: cluster/scale.

## Goal

Remove per-index resource floors and deliver safe hot-partition growth.

## Completion prerequisites

M07, M09, M10

Task-level dependencies determine when individual tasks can start. Milestone prerequisites gate completion.

## Definition of done

- Idle million-tenant catalog model has no per-idle-index worker/timer/group requirement and measured bounded enumeration/activation.
- Hot shards split/rebalance under writes with fenced routing transitions and preserved PIT/replication retention.
- Physical journal/query resources scale with active capacity, with skew/fairness/admission evidence.
- Idle/sparse/active/skewed tenancy, per-zone duplicate warmth and recovery traffic meet the selected profile cost envelope, not just a throughput target.

### M13.01: Implement paged catalog and cold activation budgets

Status: **done**. Owner role: cluster/scale.

Goal: Make idle tenancy cheap and activation bounded.

Depends on: M03.05, M02.08, M05.01.

Deliverables:

- Catalog partition strategy, lazy engine lifecycle, active caps and footprint simulator
- Re-key the catalog model on the index (tenant = index) and rerun its fixed-work gate at 100x the current idle count before any figure is quoted at PR-01 scale (M02.06 C8)

Validation:

- 1M tenants/50M-index model with >=99% idle, burst activation and catalog repair.
- Model 10M indexes with 90% idle and the large-index threshold from M02.05 (PR-01); a one-document index costs no steady-state requests (PR-02)

Definition of done:

- No O(total indexes) per-node polling/resource cost; model assumptions and real tested sizes are separate.
- Paged catalog/cold-activation mechanisms land before standalone/distributed API integration; later M13 tasks still own full elasticity/fleet proof.

Evidence:

- [evidence/m13-01-activation.md](../evidence/m13-01-activation.md): Provisional (RustFS 1.0.4, one host). Catalog partition strategy (bucket_bits_for: at most 1,024 names a page on average, pages exist only once written) and a capped lazy activation controller in hsearch-lane (ActiveSet: active/byte/loading caps, one load per cold index, LRU eviction of unleased indexes, refusal instead of growth, O(1) work independent of the idle population; 12 tests written first, seven mutations killed; a load nobody answers is reclaimed). Measured: idle indexes cost 0 requests and a node holds 4,096 entries (about 0.8 MiB) at 10M idle indexes against 784 MiB for a per-index registry; a cold activation is 2 GET + 2 HEAD (p50 about 5 ms), a hit 0, a burst of 1,000 cold indexes through caps of 64 active and 8 loading peaked at 64. C8: the catalog model re-keyed on the index passes its fixed-work gate at 100,000 vs 1,000,000 idle indexes (2 statements, 17 rows, indexed plans, RSS ratio 1.003) and both scan controls (one holding rows, one streaming them) refuse. Not done: a one-document index's steady-state requests (PR-02, needs the engine), real engine open/close (M04/M06/M07), a lane-head cache, re-splitting a catalog, sizes beyond 1,000 indexes on a real store; 50M-index figures are arithmetic.

### M13.02: Implement tenant fairness and hot placement

Status: **done**. Owner role: cluster/scale.

Goal: Isolate cache/CPU/I/O and avoid hotspot collapse.

Depends on: M13.01.

Deliverables:

- Per-tenant admission, weighted placement, cache quotas, load feedback and bounded subscriptions

Validation:

- One hot tenant with many small tenants; cold cache storm, skew and AZ imbalance.

Definition of done:

- Tenant SLO interference and rejected work are measured; moving compute doesn't change authority.

Evidence:

- [evidence/m13-02-fairness.md](../evidence/m13-02-fairness.md): Provisional (deterministic model, one host, no store). Per-tenant weighted admission (hsearch-lane admission: guaranteed share, borrowing only above a reserve, refusal by reason, entries only while work is in flight), weighted rendezvous placement with integer-only logarithm (proportional to weight, 0 of 20,000 unaffected tenants moved when a node left, replicas append only, zones before repeats, load factor), load feedback with deadband, per-tenant cache floor and ceiling quotas, and bounded subscriptions (total, per subscriber, per index). 27 tests; 13 hand mutations, 12 killed and one equivalent; an independent review found and fixed tie-breaking by slice order, an O(tenants) quota scan and a deadband pin. Measured: one hot tenant among 999 small ones refused 76.3% of the small tenants' requests under one global cap and 0% under fair admission, at 91.7% node utilisation with a 10% reserve (7.9% hot served at a 50% reserve, 51.9% utilisation: the reserve is a setting); a cold storm took the small tenants' cache hit ratio to 82.5% under one shared LRU and held it at 100% under quotas; one placement costs 2.0 us at 100 nodes, 17.7 us at 1,000, 175 us at 10,000 (about 17 ns a node); placement makes 0 store requests and leaves a real structural head byte-identical. Not done: wiring to a node (M07.08), the engine cache's own tenant LRU (M04.05), real AZ latency and inter-AZ cost, per-tenant latency interference. Zone spread over unequal zones overloads the small zone (1.69x mean at 20/20/10).
