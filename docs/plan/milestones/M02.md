## Definition of done

- Pinned Lucene Rust opens/indexes/updates/deletes/commits/refreshes through native Rust with independent correctness evidence.
- Remote-read strategy is proved on affected codecs or engine patches are concretely scoped; materialization alone cannot close the range-native requirement.
- API/workload inventory, failure-model SLOs and candidate decisions have measured baselines and explicit rejection criteria.
- Workload/SLO freeze includes profile-specific API/network/KMS cost envelopes and no unpriced or unproven failure-domain assumptions.

### M02.07: Calibrate already-selected cost profiles against workload targets

Status: **planned**. Owner role: cost/architecture/operations.

Depends on: M02.01, M02.04.

Deliverables:

- Versioned provider/region/path/unit rate-card schema, workload distributions and matched object-economy/object-resilient/latency-ha/optional latency-zonal envelopes
