- Pinned Lucene Rust opens/indexes/updates/deletes/commits/refreshes through native Rust with independent correctness evidence.
- Remote-read strategy is proved on affected codecs or engine patches are concretely scoped; materialization alone cannot close the range-native requirement.

- Workload/SLO freeze includes profile-specific API/network/KMS cost envelopes and no unpriced or unproven failure-domain assumptions.
