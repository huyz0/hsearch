### Original duration and supported continuity

The Linux design records original integer CLOCK_MONOTONIC and CLOCK_BOOTTIME endpoints under the unchanged admitted duration and retains the original wall endpoint. Require the bounds conjunctively; equality is expired. MONOTONIC excludes suspend, while BOOTTIME includes it. Wall time can shorten validity, never extend the original endpoints. Per-command time is clipped to positive remaining original time and other limits. Fresh recovery must not use `now + duration` or `now + wall_remaining`.

Trusted boot identity AND the actual trusted time namespace/clock domain must match at proof-load bookends. Prefer a qualified initial host time namespace whose lifetime spans the boot. A bind-mounted proc file or namespace inode/device alone after namespace destruction/reuse is not enduring identity. Workers do not reinterpret host ticks in a different container clock domain. Preserve observed terminal expiry so wall rollback or a fresh process cannot reopen it; sampled clock reads do not prove that every unobserved wall jump was absent.
